Claude Analytics App

A personal, single-user application operated by its owner, Olga Nikitina. It connects only to the owner's own accounts to support personal work:

  • Instagram — reads the owner's own posts, insights and comments for personal content analysis.
  • Google Calendar — reads and creates events in the owner's own calendar for personal planning.

No data is shared with third parties. The app is not offered to other users.
